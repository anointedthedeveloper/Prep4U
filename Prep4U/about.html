Our Story

Founded in 2024, Prep4U emerged from the need for a more efficient and effective exam preparation platform. Our team of educators, developers, and designers came together to create a solution that addresses the challenges faced by both students and educational institutions.

We understand that traditional exam preparation methods can be time-consuming and often fail to provide personalized feedback. That's why we built Prep4U - to empower students with the tools they need to succeed.
