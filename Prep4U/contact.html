Frequently Asked Questions

How do I reset my password?

Click on "Forgot Password" on the login page and follow the instructions sent to your email.

Can I use Prep4U on my mobile device?

Yes! Prep4U is fully responsive and works perfectly on all mobile devices and tablets.

How are the exams graded?

Exams are automatically graded instantly upon submission, with detailed feedback provided for each question.

Can I request a specific exam topic?

Absolutely! Contact us with your specific needs, and we'll work to add it to our question bank.

Is there a free trial available?

Yes, we offer a 7-day free trial for all new users to explore our platform features.

How do I cancel my subscription?

You can cancel your subscription at any time from your account settings with no cancellation fees.
